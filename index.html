About

I’m a first-year PhD student in Computer Science & Communication Studies at Northwestern University. My work in human–computer interaction centers on one question: How can we support more equitable and accessible social participation for marginalized and vulnerable groups?

By social participation, I mean more than access to services or infrastructure – I mean creating meaningful connections between people in order to make social support and safe identity disclosure happen.

I approach this across three connected layers of accessibility, social interaction, and the broader social and structural contexts that shape them, asking not just whether a technology is technically accessible, but whether it is genuinely built around the realities of the people who interact with it.

Outside research, I enjoy reading science fiction 📚, watching movies 🎥, and dancing 💃!
