About my research

As technology reaches deeper into everyday life, the harder design question is no longer whether a system works—it’s whether everyone can take part in the life it shapes, including the people it tends to reach last.

My research studies and designs interactive systems that support more equitable social participation for marginalized and vulnerable groups. By social participation, I mean more than access to services or infrastructure— I mean the everyday experiences of belonging: appreciating art, staying active, using technology on one’s own terms.

I treat accessibility, social interaction, and the broader social and structural contexts that shape them as one connected problem, asking not just whether a technology is technically usable, but whether it genuinely helps people take part in the life of a community.

Threads I’m pulling on

Thread 01

Trust & understandable systems

Making a system’s perception, intent, and limits legible in the moment of use, so people can use and interact with it safely. I’m interested in how timing, tone, and transparency let trust be earned through interaction rather than assumed, especially for users in vulnerable situations.

Thread 02

Designing futures with communities

Many of the systems that matter don’t exist yet. Using speculative and co-design methods like audio design fiction, prototypes, and role-play, I help communities imagine and shape future interactions before they are built, surfacing tensions and needs that only the people who will use them can name.

Thread 03

Accessibility beyond the screen

Designing for people who don’t rely on sight. I design and evaluate interactions through screen readers, switch controls, and audio, so a system's information and intent stay legible without a visual channel rather than translated in as an afterthought.

Thread 04

Participation in shared social settings

Inclusion isn’t only individual. I study mixed-ability, mixed-stakeholder settings, like blind and sighted people sharing the same space, where marginalized users have to be included alongside everyone else, and I design for the social dynamics that decide who actually gets to take part.
